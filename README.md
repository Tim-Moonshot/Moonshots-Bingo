# Moonshots Bingo

Because apparently listening to the Moonshot Mates wasn't enough.

**Moonshots Bingo** is an unofficial fan-made game built around the recurring phrases, predictions, tangents, inside jokes, and wonderfully predictable unpredictability of Peter Diamandis and the Moonshot Mates.

Will someone defend Elon? Will Salim want six arms? Will Alex reject the premise? Will Peter bring it back to longevity?

There's a square for that.

## How to Play

1. Open the game and generate a random 5×5 card.
2. Listen to an episode of *Moonshots*.
3. Tap a square when you hear the phrase, topic, running joke, or behavior.
4. **SINGULARITY** is the free center square.
5. Complete a row, column, or diagonal and you've got Bingo.

**Your card. Your call. Close enough counts.**

This is Moonshots Bingo, not a regulatory filing. If you think it counts, mark it.

## One Card? Five Cards?

**Play one card or play several. You do you.**

Use **New Card in New Tab** to generate another independent card. More cards means more action — and perhaps a better chance of achieving abundance before the episode ends.

## Bring Other People Into It

**Share to Play** sends someone a playable copy of your card.

**QR Code** lets someone nearby scan the card and open a playable copy on their device.

Or just send people to the game and let everyone generate their own cards.

## Post Your Results

**Take Screenshot** creates an image of your marked card, including the Episode field, so you can post it, text it, or preserve documentary evidence that someone actually said it.

## When You Win

**BINGO — We've achieved abundance!**

Expect confetti.

## What's on the Cards?

Version 1 has a pool of **41 recurring Moonshots phrases, topics, behaviors, predictions, and inside jokes**. Each card randomly selects 24 of them and puts **SINGULARITY** in the center.

Some squares are common. Some are less common. Some may sit there taunting you for two hours.

That's Bingo.

## About This Project

I'm Tim. I'm 59 years old and I sell HVAC, plumbing, and PVF products for a living.

I'm not a software developer. In fact, **Moonshots Bingo is the first coding product I've ever built — and my first vibe-coded product.**

I'm a regular *Moonshots* listener, and the idea started with a simple question: *What would be on a Moonshot Mates bingo card?*

From there, I used AI to research dozens of episode transcripts, identify the recurring phrases and running jokes, develop and play-test the game, and turn the idea into the web app you're looking at now.

And that's really why I'm sharing it.

We spend a lot of time hearing about what AI is going to enable someday. But this is something it enabled **me** to do today. I'm a 59-year-old sales guy who had an idea and, without knowing how to code, turned it into a working product.

**Is Moonshots Bingo going to change the world? No. But the fact that I could build it just might be a little bit of a moonshot.**

So yes, apparently I'm a vibe coder now.
